Map assets

drawn, not traced
the river
2.2px river over a 7px bank
amplitude ±13 about the column centre
label: Newsreader italic 15 / 3.2 tracking / link at 62%
repeats every ~1100px of path
mobile: 2px over 6px, ±3, no label

It runs the length of every page: out of the mountains in the masthead, down the left gutter on the article axis, off the bottom edge below the footer. Both old sites drew the Anduin as a coloured line across a map — in 2004 coral, in 2005 blue with its name set along it. This is that, doing a job.

the terrain
ink contour at 11.5% (masthead), 9% (footer)
1px stroke, no fill
masthead 168 tall, footer 150, mobile 130

Read west to east: a massif with a spur running off it, the plain, the river crossing, rising ground, then a range running north–south off both edges with a valley cutting west into it — contours pointing upstream, which is how a map draws one. Same profile as the ground between Minas Tirith and Minas Morgul. Nothing is traced from Tolkien's map and no place names appear on it.
